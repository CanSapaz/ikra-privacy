Ikra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Ikra ("the App") is a free mobile app that offers prayer times, the Qibla direction, a dhikr counter and similar tools for daily worship. Your privacy matters to us, and this policy explains what data is processed and how.

Summary

  • Ikra does not ask for an account or sign-up.
  • Your worship records (dhikr, prayer records, settings) are stored only on your device; we do not collect or sell them.
  • To keep the App free, it shows ads (explained below); you can remove all ads with Ikra Pro.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads in Ikra are served through Google AdMob. To serve ads and prevent fraud, Google may process your device's advertising identifier and technical information such as your IP address. This processing is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

AdMob also lets the following ad networks bid for the same ad space (AdMob mediation): AppLovin, Liftoff (Vungle) and Meta Audience Network. When an ad comes from one of these networks, technical information such as the advertising identifier and IP address may be shared with that network to serve the ad, under that network's privacy policy: AppLovin, Liftoff, Meta.

  • Ad types: a fixed banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen interstitial ads between pages, and rewarded ads that you watch only if you choose to (for example, for a 24-hour Pro trial).
  • No ads are shown in the first 24 hours after you install the App.
  • Users in the European Union and the United Kingdom are shown Google's consent form (UMP).
  • On iPhone, the App asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you do not allow it, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized.
  • For users who buy Ikra Pro, the ad technology is turned off completely; no ad requests are made.
  • Ikra never uses or shares your worship records (prayer records, dhikr and so on) for advertising.

Location

The App asks for access to your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is processed only on your device and is never sent to a server. You can also use the App without location permission by choosing your city manually.

Microphone (counting dhikr by voice)

The "Count by voice" feature in the dhikr counter is optional and works only when you turn it on. While it is on:

  • The App does not record audio; it does not transcribe, recognize or analyze what you say.
  • Only the momentary sound level is measured; whether a sound was made is used to advance the counter and is discarded immediately.
  • No audio data leaves your device: nothing is sent, uploaded to a server or stored.
  • The microphone turns off immediately when you turn the feature off or leave the dhikr counter screen.

Microphone permission is requested only the first time you turn this feature on. If you do not allow it, every other part of the App keeps working normally.

Data stored on your device

Your dhikr counts, prayer, khatm and qada records and your settings are stored only on your phone. If you use the "Backup" feature, this data is exported to a file you choose, and that file is entirely under your control. When you uninstall the App, all data on the device is deleted.

Internet use

The App's core features work offline. The internet is used only in these cases:

  • Listening to the Qur'an: audio files are streamed from Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network) servers.
  • Longer surah texts and translations: downloaded from AlQuran.cloud (api.alquran.cloud) the first time you open them and stored on your device.
  • Diyanet timetable (Europe): for cities where the App uses the timetable published by Turkey's Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet), the city's yearly timetable is downloaded from namazvakitleri.diyanet.gov.tr and stored on your device. Only the city number is part of the request.
  • Transparency data: the support goal counter, the anonymous list of contributions and the income and expense table are downloaded as a small file from the developer's GitHub Pages site (cansapaz.github.io). The file is only read; the App sends no data. The list shows no names, accounts or identities, only the package, amount and date.

During these requests, as is inherent to the internet, the servers involved see your IP address; the App adds no personal data or identifier to these requests.

Notifications

Prayer time, Friday and religious day reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no server is used to send notifications.

In-app purchases

Ikra Pro and voluntary support payments are processed through Google Play or the App Store. The App does not see or store your payment details (card and so on); transactions are subject to Google's or Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal data from any user.

Changes

When this policy is updated, the new version is published on this page.
